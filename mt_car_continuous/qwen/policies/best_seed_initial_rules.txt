1. **If the car is at the bottom of the valley (low position and low velocity):**
   - **Then apply a strong positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.8`

2. **If the car is moving towards the right hill but has not yet reached it (positive position, positive velocity):**
   - **Then maintain or slightly increase the positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.5 + 0.3 * (velocity / max_velocity)`

3. **If the car is near the top of the right hill but has not yet reached it (position close to 0.45, positive velocity):**
   - **Then apply a moderate positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.2 + 0.3 * (velocity / max_velocity)`

4. **If the car is moving towards the left hill or is at the top of the left hill (negative position, negative velocity):**
   - **Then apply a strong positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.8`

5. **If the car has high positive velocity but is not near the goal:**
   - **Then reduce the positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.2 + 0.1 * (velocity / max_velocity)`

6. **If the car is at or very close to the goal (position >= 0.45):**
   - **Then apply a small positive acceleration:**
   - **Action:** `0.1`
